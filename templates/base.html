Welcome to RAMONET
Platform Overview

RAMONET is an enterprise-grade web-based network simulation, configuration, learning, and physical planning platform. Explore the 5 integrated pillars:

1. Network Designer
Cisco Packet Tracer-inspired drag & drop topology canvas with packet flow tracer, cable validation, and live simulation.
2. RAMONET Academy
14 comprehensive interactive courses, 27 device guides, hands-on lab workspaces, and gamified progress tracking.
3. Physical Rack Designer
Plan 19-inch equipment racks (12U/24U/42U), front/rear elevations, heat dissipation (BTU/hr), and port patch schedules.
4. CCTV & IPAM Suite
Surveillance bandwidth, PoE power budgeting, NVR channel calculations, and central IPv4 VLSM subnet planning.
