Exam Subnetting Quick Trick

To find the subnet block size: 256 − (Interesting Octet Mask) = Block Size.
Example: For /26 (255.255.255.192): 256 − 192 = 64.
Subnets increment by 64: .0, .64, .128, .192.

TIA/EIA-568-A vs TIA/EIA-568-B RJ45 Twisted Pair Wiring Standards
T568BCommercial Standard (Most Widely Used)
  1. Pin 1: White / Orange (Tx+)
  2. Pin 2: Solid Orange (Tx-)
  3. Pin 3: White / Green (Rx+)
  4. Pin 4: Solid Blue (PoE+)
  5. Pin 5: White / Blue (PoE+)
  6. Pin 6: Solid Green (Rx-)
  7. Pin 7: White / Brown (PoE-)
  8. Pin 8: Solid Brown (PoE-)
T568AGovernment & Legacy Standard
  1. Pin 1: White / Green
  2. Pin 2: Solid Green
  3. Pin 3: White / Orange
  4. Pin 4: Solid Blue
  5. Pin 5: White / Blue
  6. Pin 6: Solid Orange
  7. Pin 7: White / Brown
  8. Pin 8: Solid Brown
Straight-Through vs Crossover: Straight-through uses T568B at both ends (PC to Switch, Switch to Router). Crossover swaps pairs 2 & 3 (T568A on one end, T568B on the other). Modern NICs use Auto-MDIX to auto-detect.
