Straight-Through vs Crossover Rules

Straight-Through: Both ends wired as T568B (Used to connect PC to Switch, Switch to Router).
Crossover: One end T568A, opposite end T568B (Legacy connection between Switch to Switch, PC to PC). Modern switches use Auto-MDIX to automatically resolve crossover needs.
